# Mangosteen tree (cây măng cụt), v658

Drawn from the real orchard tree (Lái Thiêu, Cái Mơn) on 2026-11-04, alongside the durian and the orange. Before/after
picture: `../../xem/3cay-truoc-sau.jpg`.

- **Shape 0:** a tidy, dense bell-shaped crown that comes down nearly to the ground.
- **Shape 1:** a taller, round-topped cone over a clear trunk.
- **Wood:** the durian kit's (`../saurieng`), with one leader and level branches.
- **Leaves:** big, thick, glossy oval leaves with a short point, in opposite pairs at the twig ends. Twigs under the
  crown reach out level, so no leaves dangle below the skirt.
- **Ripe:** 12 or 13 deep-purple round fruit, each with a cap of four thick green sepals.
- **Unripe:** pale green fruit, about a third of them turning pink-red.
- **Size:** the frames are about 84×84 for shape 0 and 84×99 for shape 1. The game bb is [-47,-88,47,18] (it was
  [-40,-86,40,20]).

## Files

- `mangcut.py`: same commands as the durian kit, with the sheet `k2_mangcut.webp`.
- `patch_mangcut.py`: hooks `plants.ft_mangcut` and sets `x$.ft_mangcut=1`.
- `atlas.json`: the frame table.
