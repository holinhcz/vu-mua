# Orange tree (cây cam), v658

Drawn from the real miền Tây orchard tree on 2026-11-04. Before/after picture: `../../xem/3cay-truoc-sau.jpg`.

- **Shape 0** is a cam mật / cam xoàn tree. A short trunk forks at knee height under a dense round crown, with smooth
  bright-orange fruit.
- **Shape 1** is an older cam sành tree (Tam Bình). It is broader and lower, and one heavy front limb is held up by a
  bamboo prop (cây chống). The fruit is rough-skinned and orange, with green shoulders.
- **Leaves:** the pomelo kit's citrus leaves (`../buoi`), scaled to orange size (0.78).
- **Trunk fork:** the tamarind kit's (`../me`), with leaders that fade in out of the bark.
- **Ripe:** 17 or 20 clusters of 1 to 3 fruit.
- **Unripe:** smaller green fruit, plus sprays of white five-petal flowers with yellow hearts.
- **Size:** the frames are about 78×74 for shape 0 and 94×73 for shape 1. The game bb is [-51,-62,51,21] (it was
  [-46,-82,46,20]).

## Files

- `cam.py`: same commands as the durian kit, with the sheet `k2_cam.webp`.
- `patch_cam.py`: hooks `plants.ft_cam` and sets `x$.ft_cam=1`.
- `shots.sh`: test shots of the mangosteen and the orange.
- `atlas.json`: the frame table.
